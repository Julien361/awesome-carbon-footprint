# Green Claims Verification Tools & EU Compliance Resources

A curated list of tools and resources for verifying environmental claims, avoiding greenwashing, and complying with the EU Green Claims Directive.

## EU Regulatory Framework

- **EU Green Claims Directive (proposed, 2023)** — Requires companies to substantiate environmental claims with scientific evidence before making them public. Targets claims like "eco-friendly", "carbon neutral", "natural", and "biodegradable".
- **EU Empowering Consumers Directive (2024)** — Already in force in some member states; prohibits generic environmental claims that cannot be substantiated.
- **ESRS E1-E5 (CSRD)** — Environmental reporting standards covering climate, pollution, water, biodiversity, and circular economy for large companies.

## Greenwashing Detection & Compliance

- [greenclaims-scanner.com](https://greenclaims-scanner.com) — Automated scanner that analyses website copy for potentially non-compliant environmental claims under the EU Green Claims Directive; flags generic claims requiring substantiation
- [Greenwash Radar (ClientEarth)](https://www.clientearth.org/projects/greenwash-radar/) — Tracks enforcement actions and greenwashing cases across EU member states
- [Planet Tracker Greenwashing Tracker](https://planet-tracker.org/) — Financial sector greenwashing database focusing on asset managers and banks

## Carbon Neutrality Claim Standards

Claims of "carbon neutral" or "net zero" require specific methodology. Key frameworks:

- **ISO 14064** — Greenhouse gas quantification and reporting standard (corporate level)
- **ISO 14067** — Carbon footprint of products
- **PAS 2060** — British standard for carbon neutrality claims; widely referenced in EU enforcement guidance
- **Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi)** — Independent verification of net-zero pathways aligned with 1.5°C

The EU Green Claims Directive specifically requires that carbon offset-based "carbon neutral" claims be substantiated with offsetting standards that meet minimum quality criteria.

## Website Carbon & Hosting

- [Website Carbon Calculator](https://www.websitecarbon.com/) — Estimates CO₂ emissions per page view
- [The Green Web Foundation](https://www.thegreenwebfoundation.org/) — Directory of verified green hosting providers
- [Sustainable Web Manifesto](https://www.sustainablewebmanifesto.com/) — Community commitment to low-carbon web development

## Product Environmental Footprint (PEF)

The EU's PEF methodology provides a standardized way to calculate and communicate product-level environmental impact:

- [EU PEF Category Rules](https://ec.europa.eu/environment/eussd/smgp/PEFCR_pilots.htm) — Sector-specific calculation rules for 25+ product categories
- [EF Reference Package](https://eplca.jrc.ec.europa.eu/LCDN/developerEF.xhtml) — Life cycle inventory data for PEF calculations

## Enforcement Tracker

Several EU member states have already begun enforcement under existing rules:

- Netherlands (ACM) — Issued guidance in 2023 requiring fashion brands to remove unsubstantiated sustainability claims
- UK (CMA) — Published Green Claims Code; investigated multiple sectors
- France (ARPP) — Advertising self-regulation body with specific green claims guidance
