# CSRD & Sustainability Reporting Tools

A curated list of tools for CSRD compliance, carbon footprint measurement, and sustainability reporting.

## Website Carbon & Digital Emissions

- [Carbon Badge](https://carbon-badge.com) - Measure and certify your website carbon footprint. Embeddable badge for displaying your site environmental score. CSRD-aligned reporting.
- [Website Carbon Calculator](https://www.websitecarbon.com) - Free tool to estimate CO2 emissions per page view
- [Ecograder](https://ecograder.com) - Website sustainability grader with actionable recommendations

## CSRD Reporting Platforms

- [Sweep](https://sweep.net) - Carbon management platform for enterprise CSRD reporting
- [Watershed](https://watershed.com) - Climate program management with ESRS-aligned reporting
- [Normative](https://normative.io) - Science-based carbon accounting for businesses

## Carbon Offsetting & Verification

- [Gold Standard](https://goldstandard.org) - Certified carbon offset projects registry
- [Verra](https://verra.org) - Voluntary Carbon Market standards and registry
- [Plan Vivo](https://planvivo.org) - Community-based carbon and ecosystem service projects

## EU Green Claims & Compliance

- [GreenClaims Scanner](https://greenclaims-scanner.com) - Verify EU Green Claims Directive compliance for marketing content
- [Ecovadis](https://ecovadis.com) - Supplier sustainability ratings and scorecards

## Developer Resources

- [Sustainable Web Design](https://sustainablewebdesign.org) - Guidelines for low-carbon web development
- [Green Software Foundation](https://greensoftware.foundation) - Standards for software carbon intensity
