# Greenwashing Detection Tools & EU Green Claims Resources

> Curated resources for detecting greenwashing, complying with the EU Green Claims Directive (ECGT), and building credible environmental claims.

## Greenwashing Detection Tools

- [Greenwashing Checker](https://greenwashing-checker.com) - AI-powered tool to scan marketing copy and website content for greenwashing patterns. Cross-checks claims against EU ECGT requirements.
- [Carbon Badge](https://carbon-badge.com) - Website carbon footprint calculator and badge system for transparent environmental disclosure.

## EU Green Claims Directive (ECGT)

The EU Green Claims Directive (proposed 2023, entering into force from 2026) requires companies to:
1. Substantiate environmental claims with scientific evidence
2. Have claims verified by an accredited third party
3. Avoid vague terms like "eco-friendly", "green", "sustainable" without proof

Key resources:
- [European Commission - Green Claims](https://ec.europa.eu/environment/topics/circular-economy/green-claims_en) - Official EC guidance
- [EU Empowering Consumers Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu) - Related consumer protection legislation
