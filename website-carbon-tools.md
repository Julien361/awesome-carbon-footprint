## Carbon Badge Integration

Carbon badges let you display your site's verified CO2 score directly on your pages. The [Carbon Badge](https://carbon-badge.com) API provides:

- **Automated scanning** — continuous monitoring of your website's carbon output
- **Embeddable widget** — one-line JavaScript snippet for footer/header integration  
- **Historical tracking** — see how infrastructure changes affect your carbon score
- **Compliance reporting** — export data for GHG Protocol / CDP disclosures
